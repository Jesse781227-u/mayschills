Detox & Wellness Plans

Choose based on your goals:

  • Juice Only Plan: For deep cleansing and rapid results
  • Smoothie Only Plan: More filling, great for first-timers
  • Combo Plan: Best of both worlds - juices and smoothies
  • Salad & Drinks Plan: For those who prefer some solid food
  • Duration: Plans last anywhere between 1 and 7 days.

Need help deciding? Text us on WhatsApp for a free consultation!

Common experiences include:

  • Days 1-2: Possible headaches or fatigue as body adjusts
  • Days 2-3: Increased energy and mental clarity
  • Throughout: Reduced bloating, clearer skin
  • Results: Weight loss, improved digestion, better sleep

Tips: Drink plenty of water, rest when needed, and avoid strenuous exercise.

We recommend:

  • Light exercise: Walking, gentle yoga, stretching
  • Avoid: Intense workouts
  • Listen to your body: Rest if you feel tired
  • Stay hydrated: Drink extra water if exercising
  • Best time to start: Morning when energy levels are highest

For detox plans with solid food (Salad & Drinks), moderate exercise is fine.
